Setup steps

  1. Save the App Secret and a verify token on the Connection tab.
  2. In the Meta App Dashboard open WhatsApp › Configuration › Webhook › Edit.
  3. Paste the Callback URL and the verify token, then click Verify and save. Meta calls this CRM; it must be reachable over HTTPS.
  4. Under Webhook fields subscribe to messages (incoming messages and delivery/read statuses) and message_template_status_update (template approvals).
  5. Click Subscribe app to WABA above (or Meta's “Subscribe” in WhatsApp Manager) so events for your number reach this app.
  6. Send a WhatsApp message to your business number — it should appear in the Inbox within seconds.
